- How realtors are using AI to help make more money.

BRIEF

### Research brief
Research verified: October 8, 2026.

**Sharp angle:** The missing step between AI and higher commissions is what agents do with the time they save. Focus on turning faster marketing and follow-up into more client conversations, then measuring whether those conversations become appointments and closings. This is an editorial hypothesis, not a proven income result.

**Three supporting points:**

1. **Start with work agents already delegate to AI.** NAR's September 2026 report says that among agents using AI, 75% write listing descriptions, 56% create social posts, and 52% draft emails or follow-up. Suggested workflow: use verified property facts and actual conversation notes to draft materials, review them, then send. These adoption figures establish real uses, not sales gains. [Source 1]

2. **Recovered time is measurable; extra commissions remain unproven.** In RPR's February 2026 survey of 225 agents, 68% reported saving at least one hour weekly and 71% named time savings as AI's leading benefit. Suggested experiment: record drafting and review time before and after AI, reserve the recovered time for prospect conversations, and track appointments, signed clients, closings, and net commission after tool costs. This measurement plan is our recommendation, not a survey finding. [Source 2]

3. **Review time belongs in the profitability calculation.** In the same RPR survey, 63% named accuracy as a concern and 47% worried about misinterpreting market data. RPR offers AI-enhanced market trend reports and CMAs, illustrating use beyond copywriting. Suggested workflow: compare generated summaries against dated local data before a client discussion. Neither source establishes that AI independently improves pricing accuracy or earnings. [Source 2]

**Two sources worth referencing:**

- **Source 1:** [NAR: REALTORS® Adopt Technology to Save Time and Improve the Client Experience](https://www.nar.realtor/newsroom/realtors-adopt-technology-to-save-time-and-improve-the-client-experience-nar-report-finds), September 22, 2026. Primary association release with current AI use cases. Use the AI-user denominator for the 75%, 56%, and 52% figures. Reasons for adopting technology refer to technology generally, not exclusively AI.
- **Source 2:** [NAR: You've Tried AI, But Can You Trust It?](https://www.nar.realtor/news/real-estate-news/technology/youve-tried-ai-but-can-you-trust-it), February 12, 2026. Reports RPR's separate 225-agent survey, covering time savings, concerns, and available tools. Self-reported results; do not merge its sample with NAR's September report or treat it as proof of causation.

**One audience question:** How can I trust an AI-generated market summary enough to use it with a client? This is a paraphrase grounded in RPR respondents' accuracy and market-data concerns, not a verbatim respondent quote.

**Writer handoff:** Explain the path from task savings to client conversations to potential revenue. Label suggested workflows as examples. Do not claim agents earned more money without documented earnings evidence.
